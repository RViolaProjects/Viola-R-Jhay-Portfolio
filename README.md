R-JHAY'S PORTFOLIO

ABOUT THIS PORTFOLIO
------------------------------------
I created this portfolio as a place to bring together my academic projects, internship work, and personal programming hobbies. As a Computer Engineering graduate, I want to show what I have learned, what I have built, and how I continue to improve my skills through hands-on projects.

This portfolio is still growing. I plan to add new projects, improve existing ones, and recreate selected past projects using fictional brands and sample data when original materials or permission to share them are unavailable. I want each project to reflect both my technical skills and my willingness to keep learning.

WHY I CHOSE A PIXEL-ART THEME
-------------------------------------------------
I've always loved pixel graphics and video games. I enjoy the creativity, charm, and sense of adventure that pixel-art games bring, so I wanted my portfolio to feel like a little game world of its own. The dark retro colors, arcade-inspired design, and quest-style project cards are my way of combining that personal interest with my love for programming.

Rather than building a traditional portfolio, I chose a design that feels more like me. This represents my journey as someone who enjoys creating, exploring new ideas, and learning along the way.

MY CAREER GOAL
